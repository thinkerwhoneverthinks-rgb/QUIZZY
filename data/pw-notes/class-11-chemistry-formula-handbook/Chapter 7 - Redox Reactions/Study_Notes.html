Oxidation & Reduction - Loss of electrons (LEO) - Increase in oxidation number - Addition of oxygen / removal of hydrogen Reduction: - Gain of electrons (GER) - Decrease in oxidation number - Removal of oxygen / addition of hydrogen OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss Reduction Is Gain Oxidising agent (O.A.): gains electrons, gets reduced Reducing agent (R.A.): loses electrons, gets oxidised O.A. and R.A. always act together
