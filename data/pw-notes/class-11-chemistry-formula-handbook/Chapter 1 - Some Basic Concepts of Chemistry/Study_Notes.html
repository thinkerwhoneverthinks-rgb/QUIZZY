Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
S.No. Concept Formula Other Information
1 Mole Concept N = number of particles NA = Avogadro's number
2 Molar Mass (M) m = given mass in grams M = molar mass (g/mol) n = number of moles
3 Percentage Composition Used to find the mass % of each element in a compound
4 Empirical & Molecular Formula Empirical Formula: simplest whole number ratio of atoms Molecular Formula: actual number of atoms
5 Stoichiometry Mole ratio = a : b : c : d Based on balanced chemical equation Coefficients represent molar ratios
6 Limiting Reagent Divide moles of each reactant by its coefficient Limiting reagent is completely consumed Determines maximum product formed
7 Percentage Yield Theoretical yield = calculated from stoichiometry Actual yield = experimentally obtained
8 Molarity (M) n = moles of solute V = volume of solution in litres M.M = molar mass
9 Molality (m) n = moles of solute W = mass of solvent in kg Independent of temperature
10 Mole Fraction (χ) Dimensionless quantity Sum of mole fractions = 1
11 Normality (N) N = Equivalents of solute / V(L) n-factor = basicity (for acid) n-factor = acidity (for base) Eq. mass = M.M / n-factor
12 Parts Per Million (ppm) Used for very dilute solutions e.g., pollutant concentration in water
13 Law of Conservation of Mass Mass of reactants = Mass of products Matter is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction (Lavoisier, 1774)
14 Law of Definite Proportions A compound always contains elements in fixed mass ratio (Proust, 1799)
15 Law of Multiple Proportions Masses of B that combine with fixed mass of A are in simple ratio same mass of carbon (Dalton, 1803)
16 Gay-Lussac’s Law Volumes of gases react in simple whole number ratios (at same T, P) Applies to gaseous reactants and products At constant temperature and pressure
17 Avogadro’s Law Equal volumes of gases at same T, P contain equal molecules Molar volume at STP = 22.4 L/mol STP: 0°C (273.15 , 1 atm
18 Atomic Mass Relative atomic mass (Ar) is dimensionless 1 amu = 1 Dalton = 1 u
19 Equivalent Mass Eq. Mass = Molar Mass / n-factor For HCl: Eq. mass = 36.5 For NaOH: Eq. mass = 40
20 Standard Conditions STP: T = 273.15 K, P = 1 atm SATP: T = 298.15 K, P = 1 bar STP = Standard Temperature & Pressure SATP = Standard Ambient Temperature & Pressure
