10de Broglie's Explanation of Bohr Quantisationde Broglie condition for stable orbit:(circumference = integer multiple of λ)Using λ = h/mv:This derives Bohr's 2nd postulate from wave-particle duality.• de Broglie wavelength: λ = h/mv = h/p• For a stable orbit, an electron wave must form a standing wave around the orbit.• Condition: circumference = nλ, where n = 1, 2, 3, ...• This gives the quantisation of angular momentum naturally.• Orbits where conditions are not satisfied ⇒ destructive interference ⇒ not allowed.

12Limitations of Bohr's Model1. Valid only for hydrogen-like (single-electron) atoms: H, He⁺, Li²⁺, ...2. Fails for multi-electron atoms (ignores electron-electron repulsion).3. Cannot explain fine structure (splitting) of spectral lines.4. Cannot predict relative intensities of spectral lines.5. Violates Heisenberg's uncertainty principle (assumes definite orbits).6. Cannot explain Zeeman effect (splitting in magnetic field) or Stark effect (splitting in electric field).• Despite limitations, Bohr model correctly gives:• Ionisation energy of hydrogen (13.6 eV)• Radius of first Bohr orbit (0.529 Å)• Spectral series of hydrogen• Replaced by quantum mechanical model (Schrödinger equation).
