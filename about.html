About Quizzy

By a Student, For Students.

Hey there! 👋

I'm the face behind Quizzy. Like most of you, I spend my days buried in PDFs, test planners, and exam schedules. I realized that finding the right questions to practice shouldn't be harder than the exam itself.

Why I Built This ☕

I'm using my tech background to take the best material from complex PDFs and turn them into easy-to-use quizzes. No more scrolling through hundreds of pages or juggling multiple apps — everything you need is right here, clean and simple.

"Quizzy is my way of making exam prep less stressful and a lot more focused."
— Founder, Quizzy
