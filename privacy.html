Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Quick Summary: Quizzy does not collect personal data. No signups, no tracking cookies, no data sales. Your quiz scores stay in your browser only.

What We Collect

Nothing personal. We don't ask for your name, email, or phone. Quiz progress is saved via browser localStorage and never leaves your device.

How We Use Data

Any stored data is used only to track your quiz progress across sessions, show answered questions, and display score history.

Data Storage

All quiz data is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data will reset your progress. We have no access to your scores.

Cookies

No tracking cookies. We only store your theme preference (dark/light) and last visited subject.

Third Parties

Hosting via Kimi Page. Optional Telegram community. We don't share data with anyone.

Children's Privacy

Quizzy is intended for students of all ages. No personal information is collected from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use means acceptance.

Contact

Questions? Email contact@studycafe.edu or reach us on Telegram.
